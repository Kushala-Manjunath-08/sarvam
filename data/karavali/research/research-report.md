# Karavali (Coastal Karnataka) Heritage Research Report

## Executive Summary
Karavali represents the vibrant coastal stretch of Karnataka bounded by the Arabian Sea to the west and the Western Ghats (Sahyadri) to the east. Comprising three districts—Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, and Uttara Kannada—the region possesses a distinct cultural identity shaped by Tuluva, Konkani, Havyaka, and Beary traditions, ancient maritime trade links, estuarine ecosystems, and world-renowned temple and folk art forms.

## District Profiles
1. **Dakshina Kannada**: Headquarters Mangaluru. Famed for Tulu culture, Yakshagana Tenku Thittu, Kadri Manjunatha temple, and historical spice ports.
2. **Udupi**: Headquarters Udupi. Noted for Sri Krishna Matha, Ashta Mathas, St. Mary’s Island basaltic rock formations, and Udupi vegetarian culinary tradition.
3. **Uttara Kannada**: Headquarters Karwar. Noted for Gokarna Mahabaleshwara, Murudeshwar monumental Shiva statue, Mirjan Fort, Yana limestone monoliths, and dense evergreen forests.

## Methodology & Sources
Research was conducted using official district portals (`dk.nic.in`, `udupi.nic.in`, `uttarakannada.nic.in`), Karnataka State Tourism (`karnatakatourism.org`), Karnataka Archaeology (`archaeology.karnataka.gov.in`), and IGNCA cultural archives. All records have been verified against authoritative government and academic repositories.
