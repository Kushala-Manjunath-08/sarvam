# Source Verification - Karavali Heritage Dataset

All records in this dataset have been cross-verified against primary government portals and academic institutions:
- **Dakshina Kannada NIC**: Verified district headquarters, historical ports, and temple locations.
- **Udupi NIC**: Verified Sri Krishna Matha history and coastal geography.
- **Uttara Kannada NIC**: Verified Gokarna, Murudeshwar, and Mirjan Fort details.
- **Karnataka Archaeology**: Verified protected monuments (Kadri, Mirjan Fort).
- **Karnataka Tourism**: Verified St. Mary's Islands geological significance.
